# RXB Catalog

The RXB catalog contains real products managed by Admin and published through the Storefront.

## Catalog language

**Product**:
A priced, publishable catalog record with a brand, name, condition, stock quantity, and optional image.

**Product type**:
The catalog category that determines which technical specifications apply to a product.

**Laptop**:
A product with computer hardware specifications and compatible RAM/storage configurations.

**Accessory**:
A non-laptop product categorized as a keyboard, mouse, mouse pad, or other accessory. Accessories have no laptop hardware or RAM/storage upgrade configurations.

**Catalog stock**:
The counted quantity of complete items available for a product record.
